WORKFLOW EXAMPLES

What could we take off your plate?

Illustrative workflow ideas, not client results. The right setup depends on your tools, process, and review requirements.

Insurance · Quote follow-up

When a quote is sent, create a follow-up task and a draft reminder. Staff review the message; a reply closes the loop. Measure overdue follow-ups and time spent chasing each quote.

Law · Client intake

Collect intake details in one place, flag missing information, and route the request to the right person. Your team reviews each submission. Measure time from inquiry to a complete intake.

Accounting · Document collection

Track which documents have arrived and prepare reminders for missing items. Staff approve exceptions and sensitive messages. Measure time spent checking and chasing each client file.

No savings guarantee: results depend on the starting process, implementation, and team adoption.
